PAGE 19 — FEASIBILITY & SCALABILITY
============================================================

Title:

FEASIBILITY & SCALABILITY

Create a clean page.

POTENTIAL CHALLENGES

1. Poor Image Quality

Problem:
Faded ink, yellowed surfaces, cracks and stains.

Strategy:
OpenCV preprocessing, adaptive thresholding and contrast enhancement.

2. Limited Script Datasets

Problem:
Historical scripts may have limited labelled training data.

Strategy:
Synthetic data generation and data augmentation.

3. Complex / Archaic Language Translation

Problem:
Historical vocabulary and context can be difficult for modern translation systems.

Strategy:
IndicTrans2 with rule-based terminology support.

4. Inaccurate Metadata

Problem:
Metadata can be incorrect when OCR is fragmented.

Strategy:
Human-in-the-loop expert verification.

5. Large-Scale Search

Problem:
Thousands of manuscript records require efficient search.

Strategy:
Optimized search indexing and structured metadata.
